# Optimus Link {#optimus-link}

![Optimus Link — Your fleet. Your control.](/assets/hytale0.7/brand/banner.png)

Craft humanoid robots, link them to a Fleet Remote and control them one at a time, each with its own tools and inventory.

![Workbench, storage Chest, Assembly Dock and deployed Optimus in Hytale](/assets/hytale0.7/native/workshop.jpg)

Each robot belongs to its deploying player and links to one remote. A remote can manage several robots through its roster. Your body stays behind, exposed to normal damage while you control a robot. Select <strong>Release Control in hotbar slot 1</strong>, then right-click to return.

## Start a fleet {#start-a-fleet}

1. [Install the supplied development package](installation.md).
2. [Craft an Assembly Dock, a remote and deployment units](recipes.md).
3. [Deploy and link your first two robots](getting-started.md).
4. [Choose a robot, equip it and take control](fleet.md).

The Assembly Dock crafts equipment without fuel or power. Robots retain their own inventories when parked. This version has no charging, autonomous jobs or ownership-transfer system.
