## Optimium and Range Extenders

- Find small Optimium veins in newly generated underground stone. Mine Raw Optimium and smelt it in a normal Furnace, one raw material to one ingot.
- Craft Range Extenders at the Optimus Assembly Dock with 6 Optimium Ingots, 4 Iron Ingots and 4 Copper Ingots.
- Link one or more Fleet Remotes by placing the actual remote in the mast’s linking slot, waiting six seconds and retrieving it.
- Extend fleet coverage with a 128-block radius around each mast. Masts linked to the same remote relay through one another.
- Reach original robots beyond the usual loading distance while your body and robot are covered by a connected network. The handset shows Connecting while a covered robot loads and Out of range when no usable route exists.
- Show a taller signal mast with an angled computer, a native-style linking panel, all 36 storage slots and the hotbar visible together.
- Display steady luminous cyan connection arcs and lighting around linked masts, without flashing sky particles. Curve height scales with mast distance.

### Compatibility and upgrading

Requires Java 25 and declares Hytale `>=0.7.3 <0.8.0`; built against 0.7.3 and checked in native 0.7.4 gameplay. The CurseForge `0.7` tag groups this compatibility family and does not mean every patch was tested.

Save and back up the matching world and player data before upgrading. Replace the old Optimus Link JAR while the game or server is stopped. The JAR includes the resource pack. Existing explored terrain is preserved; find Optimium in newly generated chunks of the normal overworld.

This is a pre-1.0 project published in the Release channel. Native singleplayer playtesting covers the new ore, smelting, mast pairing, relay control, visuals and normal reload. Actual second-client multiplayer and some sound/animation cases remain under evaluation.
