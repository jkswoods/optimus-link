# Crafting and progression

Each 0.2.3-dev recipe produces **one** item. Start with an Assembly Dock, then craft remotes and deployment units in its Robotics category.

| Result | Crafting station | Ingredients | Craft time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optimus Assembly Dock | Workbench, Crafting category | 4 Iron Bars, 2 Copper Bars, 6 Wood Trunks | 3 seconds |
| Fleet Remote | Optimus Assembly Dock, Robotics | 2 Iron Bars, 4 Copper Bars | 3 seconds |
| Optimus Deployment Unit | Optimus Assembly Dock, Robotics | 16 Iron Bars, 8 Copper Bars, 4 Light Leather | 6 seconds |

For one dock, one remote and **two robots**, prepare **38 Iron Bars, 22 Copper Bars, 6 Wood Trunks and 8 Light Leather**. These totals exclude the base game's stations and tools.

## Starting material routes

| Material | Base-game route checked against the target assets |
| --- | --- |
| Iron Bar | Process Iron Ore at a Furnace; the target recipe takes 14 seconds |
| Copper Bar | Process Copper Ore at a Furnace; the target recipe takes 10 seconds |
| Light Leather | Process Light Hide at a Tanning Rack; the target recipe takes 20 seconds |
| Wood Trunks | The base game's `Wood_Trunk` resource accepts qualifying trunk materials |

The robot's deployment unit and remote each stack to one. Their roles differ: the unit is consumed to deploy a robot, while the remote retains links and opens the fleet roster.

The Assembly Dock is a crafting bench in this version. There are no tiers, modules, fuel requirements or charging upgrades. Build more robots when you need separate inventories or equipment loadouts.
